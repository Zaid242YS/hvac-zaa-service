Use the app with confidence

1. Set up and prepare

Open Settings and enter the business name, contact details and currency. Choose Load sample to explore, then Remove sample data. Start a blank draft, enter all amounts including zero, and replace scopes and terms.

2. Calculate and compare

Cost = materials + labor hours × loaded labor rate + overhead + option cost. Price = cost ÷ (1 − target margin) + additional price. Tax applies to the full subtotal. Confirm the local taxable basis before use. No interest or finance-charge calculation is included.

Options are comparisons, not automatic recommendations. Validate actual costs with the business owner. An attractive price does not establish affordability or profitability by itself.

3. Present and record a decision

Open Buyer choices, then Presentation mode. Select an option, copy or download the customer proposal, or use Print / PDF and choose Save as PDF in your browser. Customer reports exclude internal costs and notes. Presentation mode only hides the owner interface; it is not access control. Send the exported customer document, never an internal backup.

4. Save, back up and clear

Draft edits save locally when browser storage is available. Save record creates or updates the saved record. Use Records to reopen, duplicate, delete, filter and export. Export a JSON backup regularly; import only a backup from this app. Samples remain flagged even after editing; duplicate and fully replace data in a new blank draft for real work. Remove sample data deletes flagged samples. Clear all data resets this app only and requires typing CLEAR.

Operating boundaries

Designed for a single owner on one browser, with no external dependencies. No shared database, authentication, payment processing, email sending, signature verification, automatic scheduling or regulatory approval. Different browsers and devices do not share data. Clearing browser storage erases local data. Do not store payment-card, identity-document or other sensitive information. This tool uses your assumptions; it cannot guarantee profit or sales.

Creed to Commerce in practice

Purpose: serve a real need. Amanah: state the true scope and price. Evidence: enter supportable costs. Ihsan: deliver the agreed quality. Accountability: save decisions and review outcomes. These principles are implemented here as business practices, not a religious ruling or certification.
